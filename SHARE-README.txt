FlowType - talk instead of typing (Windows)
===========================================

INSTALL (one time)
1. Unzip FlowType-Windows.zip.
2. Open the FlowType folder and double-click FlowType.exe.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   (The app isn't signed with a paid certificate; that's why Windows asks.)
3. A message says it's installed. The first time, it downloads the speech
   model (~500 MB), so wait a few minutes before the first try.
   You can delete the unzipped folder afterwards.

USE
- Click where you want to type (WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Word, anything).
- HOLD Ctrl + Alt, speak, let go. Your words get typed there.
- Long message: press Ctrl + Shift + Space to start, again to stop.
- Say "new line" / "new paragraph" for line breaks, "scratch that" to undo a sentence.
- To send: end with "send it", "send it now", "forward it" or just "... Send."
  It types the message and presses Enter.
  Or turn on purple mic -> "Auto-send" to send every message automatically.

GRAMMAR & EMAILS
- Grammar is checked automatically (LanguageTool, free). Sure fixes are applied;
  unsure ones appear as a "Check:" suggestion at the bottom of the screen.
- In Gmail / Outlook your message is laid out as an email (greeting, paragraphs,
  lists, sign-off). Saying "send it" there sends the email (Ctrl+Enter).
- Both can be turned off: ^ arrow next to the clock -> right-click purple mic.

It starts automatically with Windows. To stop it: purple mic -> Quit.
